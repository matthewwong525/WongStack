# Make your first change

Lesson 2 takes a new teammate through one small, real change from their job: plan it, look at the plan, build it and save it. The lesson stops at the save, so the owner sees each new person's first change before it goes live. It is the second lesson of [a teammate's first day](README.md), after [use skills](use-skills.md).

**Open it:** type `lesson 2` as the first message of a new chat in the project. With Paseo, open a new workspace on the project and choose *New worktree* from *main* first. The assistant teaches one step, then waits for you.

## 1. Pick a real task

Pick something small that helps your own work:

- **A skill** for a task you do again and again, such as *check new orders each morning and list the late ones*.
- **A small tool**, such as a page that adds up this week's sales: a [mini app](../../stack/mini-apps.md).

No idea? The assistant suggests two or three from your page in the company knowledge and the apps you use. You pick one.

## 2. Ask for it in plain words

Say what you want, as you would to a person: *make me a page that lists this week's late orders*. The assistant starts a plan with `/plan`. It asks only what it must, then shows you a link to the plan.

## 3. Look at the plan

Open the plan's link. The page says what will change and why, in plain words. Ask the assistant to change anything you do not like; it updates the plan. When the plan is right, tap *Build it*, or say so in the chat. Do not tap *Build and publish*: this lesson does not publish.

## 4. Build it

The assistant builds the change with `/apply`. When the change has a page, it gives you a link to look at it. Try it, and ask for fixes in the chat.

## 5. Save it

The assistant saves the change with `/save`. It shows you the link to the saved change, and says who [makes it live](../../stack/employee-project.md#who-publishes): the owner or an Admin. A person who holds *Can publish* can do it later with `/ship`.

**The lesson always stops here**, even when you may publish: the owner sees your first change before it goes live. The lesson ends with `/close`, not *publish it?*

## 6. Come back to unfinished work

Work you saved but did not finish waits for you. In a new chat, type `/continue` and pick it from the list, or name it. The assistant picks it up where it stopped.
