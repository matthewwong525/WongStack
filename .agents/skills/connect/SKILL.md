# Connect

Follow [Connect your tools](../../../wiki/stack/connect-your-tools.md) for *connect my tools*, *connect Figma for Ana*, or after setup's closing report. Its rules:

- Use a browser only through `scripts/browser.mjs`, in its own pages; a link only to sign in, or to watch.
- Never type or ask for a password, a code, or a key in the chat; pasting in Access is the fallback.
- Never change Access, and publish keys only after a yes.
