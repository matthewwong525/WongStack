# Connect

For *connect my tools*, *connect my Figma*, *connect Figma for Ana*, or after setup's closing report, follow [Connect your tools](../../../wiki/stack/connect-your-tools.md). Its rules:

- Write in [plain words](../explore/references/asking-the-user.md#write-in-plain-words).
- Never ask for a key or a password in the chat; a key comes through [the key link](../../../wiki/development/secrets.md#receive-a-key-through-a-private-link).
- The person does a key's website steps in their own browser, by [the token steps](../../../wiki/development/secrets.md#api-token-website-steps).
- Open no browser and send no password link while connecting.
- Never change Access: tell the owner what to give.
