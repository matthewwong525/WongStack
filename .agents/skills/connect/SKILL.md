# Connect

Follow [Connect your tools](../../../wiki/stack/connect-your-tools.md) for *connect my tools*, *connect my Figma*, *connect Figma for Ana*, or after setup's closing report. Its rules:

- Use the person's Chrome only after their yes, only through `scripts/chrome.mjs`, and only in its own tabs.
- Never type or ask for a password, a code, or a key in the chat; [the key link](../../../wiki/development/secrets.md#receive-a-key-through-a-private-link) is the fallback.
- Never change Access.
