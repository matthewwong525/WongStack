The retained `reserved` phase plus independent actual-deployment readback narrows the failure to that interval. The private cleanup helper encountered a repository-only manifest entry after provider deletions. A repair should durably record confirmed deployment before identity checks and bound any propagation handling to identity reads, preserving exact SHA/project checks and uncertain outcomes. It must not repeat migrations, uploads, deployments or default-ref writes after an ambiguous result. Required source gates and a separately reviewed fresh live plan remain prerequisites to another provider trial.
