Tap + Note to comment. Saving a note copies all your notes. Open a drawing and tap it to see it full screen.

Why

Each plan arrives as its own link in its own chat, at a random address that changes every day, and nothing lists what is still waiting. The chief of staff's approvals and needs you questions live only inside the daily chat, so you have to find that chat to answer them. You want one place, at one address, where you see everything waiting on you and answer it, from your phone or your desk.

What Changes

  1. 1
    An inbox in your live app. It sits at /apps/inbox, behind the same company sign-in as the rest of the app, and appears in the app list on the home page. It shows what is waiting on you, in three groups: questions that need you, approvals, and plans to review. It looks like the rest of the app.

Decisions

  1. asked2026-10-22

    Asked where the inbox lives → chose a mini app at /apps/inbox in the live app, behind the company sign-in.

  2. asked2026-10-22

    Asked who sees it → chose each person sees only their own.

  3. asked2026-10-22

    Asked what goes on it first → chose plans waiting for review, chief of staff approvals, and needs you questions; other forms and pages are left out.

  4. asked2026-10-22

    Asked whether forms and passwords belong in it → recommended keeping passwords, keys and cards out for good, and leaving other forms to a later change; the plan keeps the three chosen kinds.

  5. asked2026-10-22

    Asked whether a new plan's link opens in the inbox when the app can be reached → chose the inbox only, with the tunnel as the fallback.

  6. asked2026-10-22

    Asked how the chief shows approvals and questions when the inbox is on → chose the inbox, plus one line in the chat; typing an answer in the chat still works.

  7. assumed2026-10-22

    Assumed: the computer checks the app for taps, rather than the app reaching the computer, because the app has no path back to the computer and no queue, socket or timer today.

  8. assumed2026-10-22

    Assumed: the local reply-link server does the checking whenever any item is open, not the daily watch, so plans work in the inbox without /daily running.

  9. assumed2026-10-22

    Assumed: the computer signs in as the person, through the same helper company calls use, not with the setup's service token, because items must belong to an email and that token is kept for checks.

  10. assumed2026-10-22

    Assumed: a plan is shown in a sandboxed frame with scripts allowed but no same-origin access, because the page's scripts must run but must never reach the app's sign-in or data.

  11. assumed2026-10-22

    Assumed: notes stay in the browser on the device you typed them on, as today, because saving notes in the app adds a table and sync for a gain nobody asked for.

  12. assumed2026-10-22

    Assumed: the computer checks every 10 seconds and the open inbox refreshes every 15 seconds, because that feels immediate after a tap and stays far inside the Workers free request allowance for a small team.

  13. assumed2026-10-22

    Assumed: the inbox shows Computer offline when the computer hasn't checked in for 60 seconds, because six missed checks means it is gone, not slow.

  14. assumed2026-10-22

    Assumed: a tap waits until the computer returns and can be cancelled, and is never sent after its item closes, because a Build and publish that fires hours later is the risk to guard against.

  15. asked2026-10-22

    Asked on the review page to make a plan last 24 hours → a plan's link and its inbox item now stay open 24 hours, in the inbox and at a tunnel link alike.

  16. asked2026-10-22

    Asked on the review page that key, password and form pages last 24 hours as well → their default is now 24 hours.

  17. assumed2026-10-22

    Assumed: the live view keeps its 8 hours, because the note named key, password and form pages, and a live view holds a browser open the whole time.

  18. assumed2026-10-22

    Assumed: a private page's closing time adds tomorrow when it closes on another day, because Open until 5:30 AM reads as already closed once a link lasts a full day.

  19. assumed2026-10-22

    Assumed: only a plan's review page opens in the inbox, found by the review kit's first line, because another page sends through the tunnel's own addresses, which a sealed frame can't reach.

  20. assumed2026-10-22

    Assumed: the computer claims each tap it collects, and one whose result never comes is failed after 2 minutes rather than handed out again, because a tap that fires twice is worse than one the person copies into the chat.

  21. assumed2026-10-22

    Assumed: the plan's page talks to the inbox only on a channel handed to its first load, and its links open in a new tab, because a link that took the frame elsewhere would otherwise hand that page the plan's buttons.

  22. assumed2026-10-22

    Assumed: the inbox stays an app the owner gives in Access, because the app has no way to give one app to everyone signed in; the update note says so.

  23. assumed2026-10-22

  24. assumed2026-10-22

    Assumed: a rebuild clears an item's last answer in the list as well as on the plan's page, and an open plan reads the list's status every 15 seconds only while an answer waits, because the old answer belongs to the version it was made on and re-reading the plan would reload the page the person is reading.
