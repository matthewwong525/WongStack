The Inbox shows only your chats

0 notes · Why · Changes · Needs · Decisions

Why

The week board grew into a second to-do list that nobody kept up. Your chats already say what waits for you.

What Changes

1

The Inbox opens on your chats.

One row for each open chat: its name, its step and its folder. A chat that waits on a review goes on top under Needs you.

Rows show no dates or time left. Below are the chats that are working, then the Archive of past reviews with its search. Schedules is one button in the top bar. There are no days, dates, tabs or backlog.

2

A schedule's run opens a chat when it has something for you.

A run that finds nothing opens nothing. A run that finds something opens one chat, named for the schedule.

3

The Valentine's email, as readers will see it.

Two versions test the subject's opener. Tap a spot on the email to note it.
