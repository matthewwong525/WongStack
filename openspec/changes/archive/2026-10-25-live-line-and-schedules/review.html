Why

Your computer asks the app for news every 10 seconds, and the morning check looks at your chats once a minute. So an inbox tap can take a while to arrive, a quick reply gets missed, and the checking runs all day even when nothing happens. Schedules live in Paseo on one computer, with no one place to see what runs next or how the last run went. The helper that does all this can start chats with full control of the computer, so whatever replaces the polling must not give the app, or anyone who breaks into it, any more power than it has today.

What Changes

  1. 1
    Taps reach your chat right away. Your computer keeps one line open to the app, connecting out, the way a browser tab does. When you tap in the inbox, the app says look now down the line, and your computer picks up the tap at once, as it does today. Nothing opens a door into your computer: no webhook, no open port, nothing waiting for a call. If the line drops, your computer goes back to checking every 10 seconds until it reconnects.
    Show drawingHide drawing
  2. 2
    The line can't tell your computer to do anything. It only ever says look now. Your computer then asks for its work, signed in as you, and acts only on what it set up itself: a tap on a page it made, sending the message it fixed for that button, or a schedule whose instructions are saved in your repo and haven't changed since you approved them. The app never sends a command, a prompt, or code. So someone who broke into the app could at most start an approved schedule early or send a tap, as they could today, and both show up on your screens.

    Your schedules, in your inbox. The inbox gets a second view, Schedules, beside your agents, since both are yours alone. Schedules still running in Paseo show too, marked Runs in Paseo. You set up or change a schedule by asking in a chat, as today. You see only your own schedules.

Non-goals: no webhook or open port on your computer; Paseo Hub stays optional and unused; private links for passwords, keys and cards keep their own tunnel; other pages a chat opens (forms, lists to approve) keep the tunnel for now; setting up or changing a schedule stays in the chat; no team-wide schedule view; starting a schedule's chat still needs a chat app that can open one, Paseo today.

Decisions

  1. asked2026-10-24

    Asked whether to plan the live line alone or with schedules → chose both together, in one change.

  2. asked2026-10-24

    Asked which clock starts a schedule → chose the app's clock, which wakes the computer over the line, over keeping Paseo's clock with the app as a board.

  3. asked2026-10-24

    Asked who sees a schedule in the app → chose only its owner, like the inbox.

  4. asked2026-10-24

    Asked how the computer's helper stays running → chose a background program that starts with the computer, restarted by any chat, over running only while a chat is open.

  5. assumed2026-10-24

    Assumed: the computer connects out over a WebSocket rather than taking webhooks or running a listening server, because an open door on a computer whose chats control the whole machine is the biggest risk, and the person stressed security.

  6. assumed2026-10-24

    Assumed: the line carries only a look now wake-up, never a tap, a command, or a prompt, and the computer then uses today's signed-in routes, because a hijacked line then gains nothing and today's at-most-once delivery stays the one path.

  7. assumed2026-10-24

    Assumed: a schedule's chat starts only from the instructions saved in the repo on that computer, checked against the approved revision, never from text the app sends, because the app must not be able to make the computer run anything new.

  8. assumed2026-10-24

    Assumed: Paseo Hub is not used, because it is another company's service that would get rights to start chats on the computer, and WongStack works without Paseo.

  9. assumed2026-10-24

    Assumed: the open inbox joins the same line, because the page polls taps every 3 s and its list every 15 s, so instant delivery would otherwise show late.

  10. assumed2026-10-24

    Assumed: Paseo schedules show in the Schedules view as Runs in Paseo, read-only, and move only when asked, because the page should be the one place to look and the update must not move a job by itself.

  11. assumed2026-10-24

    Assumed: the tunnel stays for private links and for pages other than plans, because private links must never pass through the app and moving other pages is a separate change.

  12. assumed2026-10-24

    Assumed: chats report a stop through the hooks they already run, writing a local note the watch notices, with no network call, because the watch and the chats share one computer.

  13. assumed2026-10-24

    Assumed: a minor release, because it adds behavior and installs a background helper only after asking.

  14. log2026-10-24

  15. assumed2026-10-24

  16. assumed2026-10-24
