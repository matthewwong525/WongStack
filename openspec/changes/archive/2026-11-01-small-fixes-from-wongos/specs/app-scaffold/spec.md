## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The starter app opens full screen from a phone's home screen

The starter app's page SHALL let a person add it to a phone's home screen and open it full screen, with no browser bar, filling the whole screen. It SHALL keep its generic title.

#### Scenario: Opened from the home screen

- **WHEN** a person adds the app to an iPhone's home screen and opens it there
- **THEN** it opens full screen with no browser address bar, under the title *Your apps*
